The short version

Built on nights and weekends, for anyone practicing alone.

Practicing seriously, on your own, usually means owning an amp, a pedalboard, a looper pedal, a tuner, and a metronome — five purchases, five power cables, and a corner of the room that slowly turns into a gear shelf.

SoloTone starts from a simpler question: what if all five just lived in one window, on the computer that's already on the desk, and cost nothing to try?

So that's what it is — an amp and cabinet model, a pedal chain, a ten-layer looper, a tuner, and a metronome, sharing one signal instead of five separate boxes. Nothing exotic, just everything in one place.

Who it's for

Anyone practicing without a band in the room

03

Where your playing goes

Nowhere. That's the whole point.

SoloTone doesn't talk to the internet. There's no account to create, no cloud sync, no telemetry quietly reporting back on what you played or how long you practiced. It's a tool, not a subscription — everything it does happens locally, on your machine, between your instrument and your speakers.

It's still a work in progress, mostly built and played late in the evening. If something's missing, broken, or just annoying, say so — it genuinely shapes what gets built next.
